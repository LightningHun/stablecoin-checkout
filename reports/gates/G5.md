## Survivor decision

A08 disables the controller's early `disposed || gen !== generation.value` return in `accept`. It does **not** remove the reducer's generation check. Disposal increments generation before aborting; `acceptSnapshot` rejects the mismatched generation before any `sample`, payment assignment or poll scheduling. The polling catch/finally also checks disposal/generation. For the nominated stale-payment and restarted-timer behavior, removal of the early guard is redundant: the observed payment stays awaiting_payment, the signal is aborted, one GET exists and no timers remain. This explains the green mutant; it does not excuse accepting stale snapshots. M05 independently removes the actual reducer-generation rejection and is detected.

The changed internal path may perform an extra rejected-snapshot error step instead of returning early, so this is a scoped safety-equivalence finding, not a claim that every removed lifecycle guard is globally interchangeable. The original early guard remains restored. No mandatory survivor or unresolved critical test-effectiveness finding remains.
